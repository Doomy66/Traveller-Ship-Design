Ship Designer

A starship to High Guard 2022,
and the sheet the book would print.

The thirteen steps of the book's checklist, and a design sheet redrawn on every keystroke: every component's tonnage, cost and power, hull points, crew, fuel, price and upkeep, and anything that breaks a rule. Your ships are .ship files on your own machine.

    A ship saved before, as a .ship file.

  • Example ships

    Four of High Guard's own, reproduced to the ton and the credit, and a catalogue of others to open and pull apart.
